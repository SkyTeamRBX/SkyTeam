SkyTeam ROBLOX would like to congratulate Chairman and CEO Jaewoo Kim & Seokjoon Hong of Korean Air ROBLOX on their recent merger with Ja_xxon's group. This landmark agreement represents a significant step forward for Korean Air ROBLOX as it continues to expand its global presence, strengthen operational capabilities, and elevate the virtual aviation experience for all members.

We look forward to seeing the new era of innovation, collaboration, and growth that this merger will bring to the SkyTeam community. The combined strengths of both organizations promise a brighter, more dynamic future for passengers, staff, and partners across the network.

SkyTeam ROBLOX extends its full support as Korean Air ROBLOX transitions into this new chapter of excellence.

Thank you for your continued commitment to exceptional service and leadership within our alliance.
